The problem

Paid traffic keeps getting more expensive. Organic needs a face you do not have.

Every growth team knows short-form video works. The bottleneck is never the idea, it is the person in front of the camera: a founder who has no time, a creator who costs money and leaves, a schedule that turns one shooting day into three weeks of delay.

Creators do not scaleFinding five different creators for five different audience segments is harder than finding one. Each one negotiates, reschedules and eventually moves on.
Founders run out of timeThe founder is the best face for the product and the worst use of that founder's week. It works for a month, then stops.
Volume decides the outcomeOne hook in ten travels. If you can only shoot five videos a month, you never get to the one that works.

Disclosure

Every video is labelled as AI-generated

Since August 2026, the EU AI Act requires that realistic AI-generated people are disclosed at the point of contact. New York law requires the same for synthetic performers in advertising, and platforms enforce their own labelling on top.

Most teams treat this as a problem. We built for it from the start. Our characters never pretend to be a doctor, a customer or a real person giving testimony. They present your product as what they are: a brand presenter. That keeps you clear of the rules that are currently taking other campaigns down, and it is the reason our work survives a legal review.
